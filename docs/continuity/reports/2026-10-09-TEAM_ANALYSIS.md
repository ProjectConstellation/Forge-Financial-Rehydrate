# Forge financial team: independent assessment and takeover contract

Date: 2026-10-09 UTC. Engineering implementation/validation and economic achievement are separate. This assessment does not assert that every listed change has already been deployed; consult the deployment receipt and latest session journal.

## Assessment

Forge is not at maximum useful potential. It has a considerable deterministic trading/research skeleton, a functioning role tool surface, persistent workflows and market observation. Its main bottleneck is converting research spending into trustworthy evidence and executable decisions. More model calls or a more enthusiastic prompt cannot repair an incorrect cost ledger, contaminated simulator or incomplete order lifecycle.

Before the overhaul, estimated Modal monthly use was $25.5200448/$30; the reported preceding-day compute was $9.307675 and recorded inference $0.105362. Actual provider billing remains unreconciled. Real sampled treasury was about $28.27, LIVE disabled and sampled LIVE income zero. Paper results were negative. These snapshots do not establish income sufficient to cover operation. The shared provider balance cannot be attributed to Forge alone without identifying other key consumers.

Economic goals remain at least $1/hour combined, with $0.50/hour progress floor. At $28.27 capital, $24/day is roughly 85% of capital daily before compounding; it is a demanding objective unsupported by current evidence. Consistent positive income is an empirical target. A profitable average does not establish positive earnings each hour. Track rolling net result, hourly coverage, drawdown, capital capacity and all operating costs.

## Intelligence economics and scalability

Recovery pacing distributes available verified inference credit above the existing reserve across seven days. Durable reservations account for simultaneous callers and retain uncertain spend after interrupted requests. Urgency prioritizes existing work and cannot invent money. Generic cloud bursts are replaced by a specific experiment contract: objective, evidence version, decision, abort condition, timeout, worst-case bound and consumer. The $30 monthly Modal ceiling is preserved. Failures retain conservative cost uncertainty; estimates are never labeled provider invoices.

Throughput scales with real settled net earnings and cost coverage. Deposits enlarge capacity but do not prove ROI. Paper and shadow profits never authorize accelerated spending. Verified net-positive coverage can shorten inference runway to four, 2.5 or 1.5 days, and a reinvestment share of actual net profit adds budget. This can finance stronger models and larger bounded experiments as the economics support them. Pro/high is available for valuable deep decisions; routine workforce remains Flash/high. PI stays separately configured Pro/high.

Inference-assisted arbitrage is research economics: dollars spent on cognition must produce additional executable, net-positive decisions. It is not automatic arbitrage between token spending and promised market returns. Compare a cheap baseline with each paid experiment and count the downstream net outcome once. Attribution remains pending where no valid cash outcome exists.

## Portfolio and training priorities

Alpaca: use official account/assets/calendar/order docs and explicit paper endpoint. Training NAV tracks real treasury scale rather than simulated $100k capacity. Fixed relative momentum uses next-open entry and five-session hold with a conservative round-trip cost assumption and separate forward partition; it is a research baseline, not proven edge. Build and prove the deterministic paper adapter, exit and reconciliation before paper orders. Verify fee, data, fractional and extended-session permissions. Funding does not replace live readiness.

Kalshi: preserve research across event markets with current authentication/funding status, independent calibration and fee-adjusted executable price. Trading generally supports the week but has scheduled maintenance; resolution and cash settlement are different. Investigate mechanisms only where private execution and usable funded capital can support them. Odds/news feeds remain data unless a lawful supported execution rail is actually connected.

Coinbase spot: fee-aware swing/carry or other causal mechanisms must beat current observed costs. Stable triangle: known contaminated evidence is excluded from profitability frontiers until a clean independent replacement is verified. Do not let expired memory automatically rehabilitate old data. Yield: estimate separately; count only verified reward changes as realized income.

## Success criteria and limits

Tests prove invariants and implementation behavior. They do not prove profitable algorithms. First require truthful net accounting, attributable paid work, viable order lifecycle and fresh forward evidence. Then evaluate 24h/7d net cash after all costs, $0.50/$1 hourly coverage, drawdown, independent sample growth, contribution by strategy and spend per useful decision. Scale profitable validated mechanisms; retire failed mechanisms with evidence and propose genuinely changed successors.

LIVE stays disabled throughout this overhaul. Current lack of verified income, missing Alpaca execution integration, unresolved historical artifacts/billing and future forward observation must remain visible in continuity. No claim of maximum potential, guaranteed returns, or recouped compute is justified yet.
