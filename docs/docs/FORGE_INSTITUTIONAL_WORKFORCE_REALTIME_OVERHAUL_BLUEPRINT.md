# 10. REDESIGN THE WAKE GATE

The old `meaningful OR quiet_h` gate is not sufficient.

Use a predicate like:

```text
WAKE if:
    OPEN critical/high directive exists
 OR required handoff is waiting on role
 OR role SLA is near breach
 OR role SLA is breached
 OR economically meaningful owned event exists
 OR role-owned metric deteriorated materially
 OR pre-LIVE proof gate owned by role is blocking
 OR founder/Luke explicitly requested role
 OR scheduled deep-review interval is due
 OR firm under recovery threshold AND role has an actionable lever
```

Only SKIP when:

```text
no actionable event
AND no pending required handoff
AND no owned blocker
AND role is within SLA
AND a valid current IDLE_BY_DESIGN / WAITING state explains inactivity
```

Quiet-hour logic must never suppress an urgent event, critical directive, required handoff, or SLA breach.

Avoid event-flood wakeups by deduping, aggregating, and scoring events.
