1. Spend admission/stabilization and source/provider cost audit. Pass: no new generic burst; all paid paths budgeted; failed work accounted; actual/estimated/subsidized cost distinct.
2. Canonical financial/evidence truth and existing defect repairs. Pass: same windows/books yield matching answers; contaminated claims ineligible; risk visibility restored.

4. Efficient experiments and multi-portfolio integration. Pass: bounded novel feasible tests, correct fills/fees/capacity, shared money constraints and truthful rankings.
5. Luke/dashboard/continuity automation and phased production observation. Pass: fresh natural-language answers and actual campaign closure; change receipts; safe recovery.
6. Sustained 24h/7d economic evaluation. Track actual net $/hour, fraction of hours ≥0.50/≥1, worst hour/day, drawdown, net cost coverage, independent forward evidence, time-to-action, dollars spent per useful decision and total attributed operations costs. Threshold attainment is measured, never presumed from health/tests.

Scope commits to implement and validate the machinery; no claim a bounded overhaul alone guarantees income, repays sunk costs immediately or supplies missing live/funding authorization. A bottleneck that is capital, absent execution eligibility or no edge must be exposed honestly with the next concrete experiment/action.
