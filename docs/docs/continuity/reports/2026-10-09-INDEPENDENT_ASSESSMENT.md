# Imagine Forge independent assessment and takeover plan

Date: 2026-10-09 UTC. Basis: remote production source, governing documents, services, Git state and authenticated read-only API snapshots. This is a engineering/economic assessment; full deployment, execution, transfer and statistical audits remain pending.

## Judgment

Forge is not at maximum potential and is not currently demonstrated self-sufficient. It has substantial real infrastructure: live data, declarative strategy evolution, deterministic money controls, paper/shadow rails, elastic research, institutional roles, durable handoffs and a founder interface. That foundation is worth preserving. The binding problem is translating activity into trustworthy, executable, net-positive earnings. More roles, prompts, candidate volume or dashboards will not solve unreliable accounting or invalid evidence.

The system is operationally active, but financial success is not established. The sampled last-24h LIVE capital-income partition showed zero realized income, paper equity declined, and compute/inference costs were nonzero. The ~0.039/h “proven” frontier is hypothetical, largely supported by disputed stablecoin shadow evidence. No honest assessment can describe that as a working 0.50/h or 1/h income engine.

## Target and capital mathematics

Latest founder intent: aggregate ambitious operating floor 0.50/h, primary ambition 1/h and above, pursued around the clock. These equal 12/day and 24/day. Against reported real treasury 28.27, they correspond to approximately 42.4% and 84.9% of starting capital per day before compounding. This arithmetic does not prove impossibility for every business/revenue model; it shows why reliable investment-only attainment at this balance is not supported by current evidence.

Treat the goals as demanding service objectives and openly measured misses, not guaranteed investment returns or pressure to force orders. Report realized net/hour, rolling 24h/7d, fraction of hours above 0.50 and 1, worst hours, drawdown, tail losses, earnings coverage of operating costs, and available capital. Reinvestment may scale successful rails, but hypothetical simulation PnL, deposited capital and grants/credits are not earned income.

At a hypothetical 3.75% annual yield, roughly 116800 principal produces 4380/year (0.50/h), and 233600 principal produces 8760/year (1/h), before costs and changing rates. At the current balance passive yield is a liquidity parking function, not the primary target solution. Higher-return strategies need empirical evidence of capacity and risk; no assumed target return should determine leverage or position size.

The floor should apply to the total firm. Every live sleeve must justify its marginal contribution after costs/correlation; research sleeves contribute verified learning and avoided losses without pretending to be profitable investments. A portfolio can hold cash or lose on one position while still pursuing positive aggregate outcomes. Require eligibility and economic value, not profit from every individual trade or asset.

## What is strong

1. Separation of reasoning and deterministic execution: model proposes research artifacts; protected risk/execution/ledger path governs money.
2. Genome and lineage design: declarative strategies, archived failures and adaptive evolution are appropriate for continuous learning.
3. Diverse economic scope: Coinbase, prediction markets, stablecoin microstructure, equities/data and idle yield; no permanent dependence on one market.
4. Persistent workflows and memory: ownership/handoffs, negative knowledge and explicit reopening conditions can prevent repeated wasted research.
5. Dedicated host, scoped services and resource controls: sensible isolation and maintainability.
6. PAPER-first operation and founder-gated live/transfer mechanisms: foundations for progressive validation.
7. Luke as a single natural-language interface: coherent product direction if his answers and campaign closure are trustworthy.

These are architectural strengths. They are not independent proof every implemented feature meets its promised contract.

## What prevents maximum potential

### Financial truth is fragmented

Source inspection shows three different views of money. The wallet-window API labels a delta as LIVE true net without deducting externally paid inference/compute or removing timed-window deposits. The velocity realized calculator subtracts inference/membership but omits compute. Overview includes compute and paper trading with other definitions. Treasury Truth can substitute wallet NAV for missing paper NAV, declare live NAV zero because trading is disabled, and subtract paper deployment from live availability. Its reconciliation derives both sides largely from the same cached velocity values, not an independent wallet/order/ledger agreement.

Repair the invariant first: distinguish wallet truth, book PnL, external funding, actual costs, realized/unrealized value and per-rail settlement. Every caller must use an explicit scope. Show treasury net and full economic net when provider costs are paid outside the wallet. Show founder-attributed incremental costs and all-in owner cost as separate views. Avoid double debiting fees already embedded in equity/fills.

### “Proven” evidence can remain economically ineligible

Triangle is CONTAMINATED in opportunity memory while the frontier still presents it as EXCEPTIONAL/proven. Officer reports dispute fees, quote completeness and calibration identity. That disagreement must change deterministic claim eligibility, rankings and Luke answers, not only generate another narrative report. Use unique instrument/strategy/evidence-version keys and invalidate dependent estimates when data changes.

Shadow profit requires realistic fillability, queue position, all conversion legs, asynchronous leg risk, per-product/account fees, inventory limits, adverse selection and settlement. On Alpaca, paper trading is explicitly simulation; Alpaca documents omissions such as latency slippage, market impact, queue position and fees. Broker paper results must be used for integration/learning and cannot establish live execution quality.

### Workflow and score integrity need hardening

advance() marks pending handoffs done before it validates payload and stage constraints. The inspected path does not first verify the current expected stage and role against the submitted stage. _on_complete() sums claimed payload income deltas without settled evidence, and observed money scorecards include incomplete/no-measure output. Text scoring can reward reports as EARNER even when no money was earned. Those are code-level validity problems, not motivation problems.

Enforce transactional ownership/stage checks and idempotency before any state mutation. Separate useful contribution, measured experiment success and realized earnings. A rejected submission must leave the handoff unchanged. A workflow cannot claim measured income without server-verified evidence. Investigate unusually short/seed-like records for provenance rather than assuming misconduct.

### Research spend is ahead of verified value

Observed monthly Modal spend is about 25.52 of 30; the 24h cost window reported roughly 9.67 compute plus 0.10 inference. Numerous job attribution entries remain pending_evidence. At a 28.27 treasury, this is materially relevant even when bills come from a separate account. Optimize intelligence: pay for a hypothesis whose answer changes a decision, avoid repeating the same failed search, reuse cached artifacts and tie every burst to lineage/outcome. Preserve useful local/low-cost observation and positive-information experiments. Do not equate “under target” with authorization to spend faster indefinitely.

### Autonomy stops short of verified closure

Core loops are alive, yet report counts and SLA labels disagree; Luke has open work; some capability boards are stale. A live credential can coexist with a board claiming no key; a Kalshi balance of zero prevents a funded live rail; Odds returned 401; Alpaca reports treasury unknown despite Forge’s reported balance. Self-healing must restore data and contracts, not just restart a process until a dashboard turns green.

## Plan to take over

### Phase 1 — establish an auditable control plane

Preserve remote uncommitted work. Continuity is installed in this session. Next implement the canonical financial contract and Treasury Truth repair; remove contaminated evidence from validated rankings; fix risk visibility. Meaningful isolated checks must cover external deposits, withdrawals, internal moves, provider costs, cost attribution, book isolation and stale/missing data. Then verify API/Luke agreement against read-only reconciled venue/ledger records.

Completion: one truthful account of where money is, what earned it, what cost it and what is unknown. Green health alone is insufficient.

### Phase 2 — make the institution accountable

Harden typed workflow transitions, result schemas and financial attribution. Consolidate SLA/report counts and role mapping. Deduplicate event wakes and bound retries. An authorized founder goal becomes a durable objective with concrete owners, deadlines and evidence; Luke follows it until verified completion or a precise blocker. Automatic engineer/deployer work must retain rollback and independent validation.

Completion: rejection leaves state unchanged; valid handoffs survive restart; no fake completion; replay is idempotent; each objective has a measured result or explicit accountable blocker.

### Phase 3 — run a portfolio of falsifiable experiments

Rank experiments across rails by net expected information value, executable capacity, independent evidence, capital lockup and correlation. Continue simultaneous scoped PAPER work where useful rather than wait for one venue to solve everything. Specify fee/capacity/fill realism and preregister out-of-sample criteria before measuring. Cross-market signals can share research, but identical event bets count as one independent exposure.

Coinbase spot: longer fee-clearing mechanisms and regime/evidence discipline; no tiny-fee-blind breakout churn. Stable rails: reconcile complete instrument/leg economics before promoting. Kalshi: verify auth/funding/mode, independent cluster evidence and settlement details; no unfunded or shadow return as live profit. Equities/Alpaca: training NAV reflects real Forge scale, broker sandbox balance excluded, session liquidity/PDT/fractional rules verified before live assumptions. Idle yield: verified eligible rewards with cost attribution and liquidity. New legitimate service/revenue rails can be researched because the capital requirement for investment-only targets is large, but external delivery/publishing/spend needs its own authorized scope.

Completion: at least one affordable, executable mechanism has genuinely new forward evidence after all costs and a known capacity curve. Failed hypotheses remain reusable knowledge.

### Phase 4 — allocate and scale progressively

One portfolio allocator considers aggregate available capital, shared reserve, venue minima, correlated bets, outstanding orders, settlement locks, inventory exposure and adverse conditions. Price the next dollar’s marginal net value. Rotate when evidence changes without constant thrashing. Scale only when net edge survives marginal execution costs and capacity; do not extrapolate a small shadow edge linearly.

Micro-live requires founder authorization and readiness. This continuity/assessment request does not enable it. Compare paper/shadow expectations to actual fills and withdraw proof if execution diverges. No transfer or risk boundary changes merely to chase targets.

Completion: venue/ledger reconciliation after actual approved live activity, cost-complete results, controlled drawdown and a tested successor/recovery path.

### Phase 5 — prove and maintain self-sufficiency

Define self-funding as reconciled earned net revenue covering attributed recurring compute, inference, data, transaction and hosting/other operating costs over sustained windows, while capital/working-liquidity protections hold. Include externally subsidized credits explicitly; credit balance is runway, not profit. Track days-to-runway, cost per useful experiment, evidence throughput, earnings coverage, hours over the targets, time to repair and capital recycle.

Run a 24h/7d scorecard after contracts stabilize, retain longer regime-spanning observations before claiming durability. Automate objective recovery when targets are missed: identify constraint, choose the experiment/action, delegate, measure and revise. No numerical timeline for hitting the target is credible until a live edge and capacity curve exist.

## Luke as advisor and operations manager

Keep Luke in his existing owner-facing Telegram profile. He should get fresh canonical numbers, answer financial questions plainly, execute authorized reversible work without repeated selection menus, delegate through the persistent institution and return verified outcomes. Money reports must label LIVE/PAPER/SHADOW, timestamps/windows, costs and uncertainty. “Transfer” is a fresh preview plus existing authenticated founder confirmation; current can_transfer=false blocks it. Inspected code supports registered owner Coinbase portfolio moves, not proof of external cash withdrawal support.

A Telegram message was not sent and no transfer path was exercised in this review. End-to-end message delivery, confirmation replay/expiry, actual portfolio permissions and post-transfer reconciliation remain verification work under explicit action authorization.

## Priority and evidence commitments

First priority: money truth and evidence correctness. Second: workflow/results integrity and cost-effective research. Third: independent experiments and shared portfolio allocation. Fourth: authorized live calibration and sustained self-funding. Preserve ambition at every step, but do not conceal misses or manufacture “success” from estimated frontier, canned role grades or simulated capital.

This plan can improve the probability of success and make failures visible early. It cannot ensure positive investment earnings every clock hour. Investment returns carry risk; the engineering commitment is a measurable, adaptive, auditable system that pursues the founder’s targets and learns from actual results.
